Siddhak Hazare Shree Khatu Shyam Spray (50 ml) is a devotional fragrance made with deep respect for Shyam bhakts. Every spray is meant to remind you of the serene ambience of Khatu Dham, so you feel closer to Shyam Baba wherever you are.

It is not just a normal room freshener. Its traditional Indian aroma turns your surroundings into a calm, devotional space for worship.

Benefits

  • Devotional, traditional fragrance inspired by the sacred environment of Shree Khatu Shyam Ji Mandir, for a temple-like feel at home
  • Made for pooja rooms, home mandirs, satsang halls, bhajan evenings and religious functions
  • Easy spray format: a few sprays freshen the room and set a devotional mood
  • Helps create a soothing, peaceful setting for prayer, meditation and mantra chanting
  • Adds a special devotional touch to festivals, vrat and special occasions
  • A meaningful gift for family, friends and fellow Shyam bhakts on festivals and special occasions

Ideal for

  • Morning and evening aarti
  • Pooja and daily rituals
  • Bhajan, kirtan and satsang
  • Meditation, jaap and path
  • Spiritual gatherings and festive celebrations
  • Homes, offices, shops, spiritual centres and vehicles

How to use

  • Shake the bottle gently before use.
  • Hold upright and spray 2–4 times into the air, away from the face.
  • Use around your pooja area, living room or office cabin before starting aarti, bhajan or meditation.
  • You can also lightly spray curtains, cushions and soft furnishings for a longer-lasting aroma.
  • Reapply as needed through the day.
