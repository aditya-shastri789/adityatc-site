Siddhak cow dung cakes (gau kande / gobar ke uple) are prepared from pure, naturally dried desi cow dung for Hindu rituals, havan, yajna, agnihotra and daily pooja.

In Sanatan Dharma the cow (Gau Mata) is deeply revered, and the five cow products known as Panchgavya (milk, ghee, curd, gomutra and gobar) are considered sacred. As per Hindu belief, Goddess Lakshmi resides in cow dung, and using it in pooja, havan and agnihotra is believed to invite her blessings. That is why village and traditional homes still use cow dung to purify courtyards on festivals like Diwali, prepare the havan kund fire and perform Agnihotra at sunrise and sunset.

What makes them special

  • Made from pure desi cow dung: no chemicals, no artificial binders, no synthetic fragrance
  • Naturally sun-dried and shaped using age-old methods
  • Made to burn steadily and evenly
  • Easy to light with a little ghee, camphor or a cotton wick

Benefits

  • Gentle, earthy fragrance that suits mantra chanting, meditation and worship
  • Traditionally believed to purify the environment on a subtle level
  • Believed to invite Goddess Lakshmi's blessings of prosperity and harmony (a matter of faith and tradition)
  • Biodegradable and eco-friendly, free from synthetic fuels
  • After use, the ash is often added to home gardens as a traditional soil additive

Ideal for

  • Havan / yajna at home, temple or ashram
  • Agnihotra (morning and evening fire ritual)
  • Navratri, Diwali, Durga Pooja, Lakshmi Pooja, Govardhan Pooja, Satyanarayan Katha
  • Griha Pravesh and vastu shanti
  • Shraddh, Pitru Tarpan and festive poojas
  • Daily pooja fire for dhupa, aahuti or simple flame offerings

How to use

  • Havan / yajna: place 2–4 cakes in the havan kund, add a few dry wood sticks or cotton wicks, light with a diya or matchstick, and once the fire is steady offer ghee and havan samagri while chanting mantras.
  • Daily pooja / Lakshmi pooja: light a small piece or one full cake in a safe fireproof container, let it settle into embers, then offer dhoop, flowers and aarti.
  • Agnihotra (with proper guidance): place a small portion in a clean copper pyramid or agnihotra vessel, light it a few minutes before sunrise or sunset, offer ghee and materials as per agnihotra vidhi, and chant the mantras.
  • Always follow the guidance of your family priest, guru or ritual expert for specific ceremonies.

Storage and safety

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture.
  • Keep away from children and pets.
  • Always burn on a non-flammable surface or in a havan kund, with proper ventilation indoors.
  • Never leave a burning fire unattended.
