Siddhak rose dhoop sticks blend the sacredness of cow dung (gomaya) with the gentle sweetness of rose. They are made to make Maa Durga, Ambe Maa, Mata Rani and Ganesh pooja feel more complete and devotional.

In Hindu tradition the cow is revered as Gau Mata, and cow dung has been used in havan, yajna and pooja since ancient times. Burning it in a ritual way is traditionally believed to purify the surroundings on a subtle level and invite blessings. Rose (gulab) is offered to Mata Ji as a symbol of love and devotion, and is used in garlands for Ganesh pooja and Ganesh Chaturthi.

Benefits

  • Pure cow dung as the main base, with no charcoal added
  • Floral, lightly sweet, soothing rose fragrance: not overly strong or artificial
  • Feels closer to havan and village-style pooja than a chemical room freshener
  • Helps you settle into a devotional mood; pairs well with flowers, kumkum and prasad
  • Suits people who prefer sattvik, cow-based products and those sensitive to harsh incense
  • 50 sticks: enough for daily aarti plus festivals and vrats

Ideal for

  • Navratri, Durga Pooja, Durga Ashtami and Vaishno Devi pooja
  • Ambe Maa, Kali Maa and Santoshi Maa pooja; Tuesday and Friday Devi pooja
  • Jagran, chowki and bhajan sandhya at home
  • Daily Ganesh aarti, Ganesh Chaturthi (sthapana, aarti, visarjan) and Sankashti Chaturthi
  • Office, shop and business Ganpati pooja
  • Gifting for Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali, Griha Pravesh, new mandirs, weddings and anniversaries

How to use

  • Clean the pooja area and arrange Mata Ji's and Ganesh Ji's idols or photos with diya, water, flowers, kumkum, rice and prasad.
  • Hold one stick at the bare end and light the coated tip with a matchstick or diya.
  • Let it burn for a few seconds until the tip glows, then gently blow out the flame.
  • Wave the dhoop in gentle circles before the deities, chanting "Ya Devi Sarva Bhuteshu..." or "Jai Ambe Gauri" for Mata Ji, and "Om Gan Ganapataye Namah" or "Jai Ganesh Deva" for Ganesh Ji.
  • Place it in a dhoop stand on a heat-proof surface and continue with aarti, bhajan or japa.

Safety and storage

  • Always place a burning dhoop stick in a stable, non-flammable holder on a heat-resistant surface.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains, paper and other flammable items.
  • Use in a well-ventilated area, especially in small rooms.
  • Never leave burning dhoop unattended.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight, and keep the pack closed to preserve the fragrance.
