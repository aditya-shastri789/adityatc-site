Siddhak Kesar Chandan freshener spray is made for those who worship Lord Vishnu, Lakshmi–Narayan, Krishna, Ram and the other avatars of Shri Hari. It brings the timeless kesar (saffron) and chandan (sandalwood) fragrance into an easy-to-use spray.

A few sprays before pooja make your space feel calmer and cleaner, whether you are doing daily Vishnu Ji Pooja, Satyanarayan Katha, Ekadashi vrat or reading the Bhagavad Gita.

Why kesar and chandan

  • Chandan is used as tilak and paste on the forehead of Lord Vishnu and devotees, and stands for coolness, purity and sattvik shanti.
  • Kesar is considered highly auspicious and is used in prasadam, abhishek and tilak during special Vishnu and Lakshmi–Narayan poojas.

Benefits

  • Warm, soft fragrance inspired by real saffron and sandalwood: subtle, not overpowering
  • Temple-like feel in seconds, without burning dhoop or incense every time
  • Helps mask everyday household smells (kitchen, closed rooms)
  • Gentle and sattvik, not like a strong synthetic deodoriser
  • Fragrance is subjective; intensity may vary by person and room size

Ideal for

  • Daily Vishnu Pooja and aarti
  • Satyanarayan Katha
  • Ekadashi and Vaikuntha Ekadashi
  • Janmashtami and Ram Navami
  • Thursday (Guruvar) Vishnu or Lakshmi–Narayan pooja
  • Before Vishnu Sahasranama, Gita path, kirtan, japa, dhyana or pranayama
  • Home mandir, living room, meditation or yoga space, bedroom or study
  • Homes and flats where you cannot burn much dhoop or incense
  • Gifting to devotees of Vishnu, Krishna or Ram, for Griha Pravesh and new mandir setups

How to use

  • Lightly tidy or dust your pooja area.
  • Hold the bottle upright and spray 3–6 times into the air, around the room and near (not on) the mandir.
  • You can lightly mist curtains, room corners and the pooja asan from a safe distance.
  • Wait a minute for the mist to settle, then light the diya and dhoop and begin pooja.
  • Repeat lightly before the next session of prayer or meditation if the room feels stuffy.

Care and safety

  • Do not spray directly on idols, photos, diyas, open flames or prasad.
  • Avoid spraying on skin, eyes or face.
  • Test on a small hidden area before using on delicate fabrics or surfaces.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat.
