Siddhak kesar dhoop sticks bring the holy fragrance of kesar (saffron) to your daily Vishnu Pooja. They combine pure kesar with a clean, natural base for a soft, soothing aroma that supports focus, devotion and inner calm.

In Sanatan tradition kesar is closely tied to Lord Vishnu and Goddess Lakshmi: it is used in Vishnu tilak, offered in prasadam such as kesar kheer, sheera or milk during Vishnu vrats, and its golden colour is linked with abundance, dharma and divine grace. Lighting kesar dhoop before offering flowers, naivedya and deepam completes the dhupa step of the Shodashopachara (16-step) pooja.

Benefits

  • Made with pure kesar (saffron) and natural base ingredients for a smooth, clean burn
  • Soft, sattvik fragrance, not harsh or heady, for closed home spaces and people sensitive to strong smoke
  • Helps create a calm setting for Vishnu Sahasranama, scripture reading or silent meditation
  • Welcoming aroma for festivals and guests
  • 50 sticks: enough for daily morning and evening pooja plus special occasions

Ideal for

  • Daily Vishnu / Lakshmi Narayan pooja and aarti
  • Satyanarayan Katha and Vishnu Sahasranama path
  • Thursday (Guruvar) pooja for Vishnu and Brihaspati
  • Vaikuntha Ekadashi, Dev Uthani Ekadashi, Diwali, Dhanteras, Janmashtami and Ram Navami
  • Griha Pravesh and other new beginnings
  • Meditation, japa and study of the Gita or Bhagavatam
  • Homes, ashrams, small temples, offices and spiritual centres

How to use

  • Clean the altar and place the photo or murti of Lord Vishnu / Lakshmi Narayan with flowers, diya, water and prasad.
  • Light one end of the dhoop stick with a matchstick or diya flame, let it burn for a few seconds, then gently blow out the flame so it glows.
  • Wave the dhoop in a circular motion before the deity (Dhupa Upachara), softly chanting "Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya" or "Om Vishnave Namah".
  • Place it in a proper stand or on a fire-safe surface, away from flammable materials, and continue with aarti or stotras.

Safety and storage

  • Always place a burning dhoop stick in a stable, non-flammable holder on a heat-resistant surface.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains, paper and other flammable items.
  • Use in a well-ventilated area, especially in small rooms.
  • Never leave burning dhoop unattended.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight, and keep the pack closed to preserve the fragrance.
