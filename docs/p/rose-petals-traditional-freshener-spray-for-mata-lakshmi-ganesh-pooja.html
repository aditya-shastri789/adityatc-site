Siddhak Rose Petals freshener spray is made for devotees of Mata Ji (Durga, Ambe, Vaishno Devi, Lakshmi, Santoshi, Kali) and Lord Ganesha. It captures the fragrance of fresh rose petals, one of the most loved flowers in Hindu worship.

Rose (gulab) is offered to the Divine Mother in garlands and decorations during Navratri, Durga Ashtami and other Devi poojas, and stands for love, devotion, shakti and beauty. For Lord Ganesha, roses are common in Ganesh sthapana, aarti and visarjan, and pair well with modak and laddoo prasad.

Benefits

  • Classic rose petals fragrance: fresh, floral and lightly sweet, not a harsh synthetic perfume
  • Prepares your pooja room before worship and helps neutralise everyday smells (kitchen, closed rooms, dust)
  • Instant temple-like ambience when guests arrive for pooja, bhajan or katha
  • Non-messy way to refresh the mandir, for flats where burning a lot of dhoop is not possible
  • Fragrance is subjective; intensity can vary by person and room

Ideal for

  • Daily morning and evening aarti
  • Navratri, Durga Pooja, Ashtami, Mata ki Chowki, Jagran and bhajan sandhya
  • Tuesday and Friday Devi pooja
  • Ganesh Chaturthi, Sankashti, Ganesh sthapana, aarti and visarjan, and new beginnings at home, office or shop
  • Diwali, Karva Chauth, Teej, special vrats and sankrantis
  • Living rooms before bhajans, meditation and japa corners, reading corners
  • Gifting for Navratri or Ganesh Chaturthi visits, Griha Pravesh and new mandirs

How to use

  • Clean the altar and arrange Mata Ji's photo or murti, or the Ganesh murti, with flowers, durva, chunri, diya and prasad.
  • Shake gently if needed, hold the bottle upright and spray 3–6 times into the air, around the room and near (not directly on) the mandir.
  • Lightly mist curtains and corners from a safe distance for a lingering aroma.
  • Wait a minute for it to settle, then light the diya and begin aarti ("Jai Ambe Gauri", "Aigiri Nandini", "Om Gan Ganapataye Namah" or "Vakratunda Mahakaya").
  • At a chowki or jagran, spray before guests arrive and lightly again during breaks.

Care and safety

  • Do not spray directly on idols or photos of deities, on diyas, candles, agarbatti or any open flame, or on prasad, food or offering water.
  • Avoid contact with eyes, mouth and skin.
  • Test first on a small hidden area of fabric or delicate surfaces.
  • Keep away from children and pets.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and heat, and close the bottle properly after use.

Pack sizes

  • 50 ml: for travel, gifting or small mandirs
  • 100 ml: for regular home use, satsang rooms and larger pooja areas
