Siddhak Panchgavya (Panchtatva) dhoop sticks bring the purity of Panchgavya to your daily pooja in an easy stick format. In Sanatan Dharma the five products of the cow, together called Panchgavya, are considered holy and are used in yajna, havan and samskaras.

What Panchgavya is made of

  • Gomaya (cow dung)
  • Gomutra (cow urine)
  • Dugdh (milk)
  • Dahi (curd)
  • Ghee (clarified butter)

Benefits

  • 100% cow dung base: pure, dried cow dung (gomaya) as the main base
  • Made with the Panchgavya elements and natural herbs, as a symbolic Vedic offering
  • Free from charcoal, harsh chemicals and synthetic binders, for a gentler, sattvik smoke
  • Warm, earthy, mandir-like fragrance that recalls temples, havans and village poojas
  • As per tradition, burning Panchgavya dhoop is said to purify the atmosphere and offering it is considered punya (a matter of faith)
  • 50 sticks: enough for daily morning and evening pooja plus festivals

Ideal for

  • Daily griha pooja and aarti
  • Havan and yajna at home, alongside havan samagri
  • Diwali, Navratri, Janmashtami, Ganesh Chaturthi, Shivratri, Ram Navami and Ekadashi
  • Satyanarayan Katha, Griha Pravesh, Vastu Pooja and Sankalp poojas
  • Meditation, mantra chanting, japa and dhyana
  • Gifting for Griha Pravesh, weddings, anniversaries, satsangs and festival visits

How to use

  • Clean the altar and arrange your deity idols or photos with diya, flowers, water and prasad.
  • Hold one stick at the uncoated end and light the other end with a matchstick or diya.
  • Let it burn for a few seconds until the tip glows, then gently blow out the flame.
  • Move the dhoop in circles before the deity to complete the dhupa step, chanting "Om Shanti Shanti Shantihi", "Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya" or "Om Namah Shivaya" as suits the deity.
  • Place it in a dhoop stand on a stable, fire-safe surface and continue your pooja.

Safety and storage

  • Always place a burning dhoop stick in a stable, non-flammable holder on a heat-resistant surface.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains, paper and other flammable items.
  • Use in a well-ventilated area, especially in small rooms.
  • Never leave burning dhoop unattended.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight, and keep the pack closed to preserve the fragrance.
