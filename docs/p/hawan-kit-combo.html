Siddhak Hawan Kit Combo brings the basic hawan essentials together in one pack, for daily hawan, Navratri puja, Diwali puja, Satyanarayan katha, Griha Pravesh or a special havan. It is made for those who value purity, simplicity and tradition and do not want to buy items separately.

What's inside

  • Hawan Samagri – 1 kg: a blend of traditional herbs, roots, dry flowers, woods and natural resins, prepared as per traditional guidelines for hawan, yajna and homam; enough for several small hawans or a few long rituals
  • Kande (cow dung cakes / uple) – 21 pieces, 2.5 inch: well-shaped and easy to light; traditionally used as fuel for hawan, yagna and agnihotra; burns steadily to keep the sacred fire going
  • Kapoor (camphor) – 50 g: to light the kande quickly and for aarti

Benefits

  • Complete basic hawan setup in one pack
  • Ready to use; helpful for busy families and first-time users
  • Hawan samagri is offered into the fire with ghee and mantras, and is believed to purify the surroundings, create a peaceful, sattvic atmosphere and support calm and spiritual connection
  • A thoughtful gift for housewarming, festivals and devotional occasions

Ideal for

  • Multiple small daily hawans or a few long, detailed rituals
  • Diwali, Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Griha Pravesh, birthdays and anniversaries

How to use

  • Place 2–3 kande in your hawan kund and light them with a piece of kapoor.
  • Once they catch flame and form embers, add hawan samagri with ghee while reciting mantras.

Pack size
