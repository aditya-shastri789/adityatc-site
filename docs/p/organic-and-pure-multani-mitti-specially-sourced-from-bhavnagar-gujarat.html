Siddhak Multani Mitti (Fuller's Earth) is specially sourced from Bhavnagar, Gujarat, a region known for fine-textured, mineral-rich clay. It is carefully processed to keep its natural properties and is not blended with low-grade clays.

It is a single ingredient: pure Multani Mitti in natural powder form, free from chemicals, preservatives, colours and added fragrance, and not mixed with talc, chalk or fillers. Its fine texture blends easily with rose water, milk, aloe vera gel or hydrosols, spreads smoothly without feeling gritty and washes off easily.

Benefits for skin

  • Deep cleanses pores by drawing out excess oil, sweat and impurities
  • Absorbs excess sebum, useful for oily and combination skin
  • Gives a fresh, cooling feel on hot, humid days
  • Helps reduce the appearance of tan and dullness with regular use
  • Supports visibly smoother, clearer-looking skin over time

Benefits for hair and scalp

  • Helps remove oil, dirt and product build-up from the scalp
  • Leaves hair feeling lighter and cleaner
  • Adds a mild natural volume to oily, weighed-down hair
  • Mix with curd, aloe vera, amla powder or herbal infusions for a home hair spa
  • Benefits are based on traditional use; individual results may vary

How to use

  • Classic face pack (normal to oily skin): mix 1–2 teaspoons with enough rose water to make a smooth paste. Apply on a cleansed face and neck, avoiding eyes and lips. Leave for 10–15 minutes until just semi-dry, rinse with lukewarm water and moisturise.
  • Glow pack (dull or tanned skin): 1 teaspoon Multani Mitti, 1 teaspoon curd or milk, and an optional pinch of turmeric or sandalwood powder. Leave for 10–15 minutes and rinse. Use 1–2 times a week.
  • Dry or sensitive skin pack: 1 teaspoon Multani Mitti with 1–2 teaspoons aloe vera gel and a few drops of honey or facial oil. Do not overuse on very dry skin.
  • Hair and scalp mask: 2–3 tablespoons with enough curd, aloe vera juice or herbal tea to make a thick paste. Apply to scalp and hair for 15–20 minutes, then rinse, with a mild shampoo if needed.

Tips

  • Always do a patch test before first use.
  • Wash off when semi-dry; do not let the mask crack.
  • Use 1–3 times a week depending on skin type (less for dry, more for oily), and follow with toner and moisturiser.
  • Suitable for women and men of all ages with oily, combination and normal skin. If your skin is very dry or sensitive, use sparingly, mixed with hydrating ingredients.

Storage

  • Store in a cool, dry place and keep the pack tightly sealed.
  • Use a clean, dry spoon and keep water out of the pack to prevent lumps.
