Siddhak Mogra room freshener spray fills your home with a gentle, devotional fragrance that recalls the gardens of Vrindavan. It is made for those who want a soft, sacred touch in their Krishna Ji Pooja and daily routine, without burning incense every time.

In Indian tradition, mogra (jasmine) is used in garlands offered to Krishna Ji, to decorate idols and jhulas at Janmashtami and special poojas, and at bhajan, kirtan and satsang gatherings. Its sweet fragrance is linked with Vrindavan and Gokul, Radha–Krishna leelas and the joy of Krishna bhakti.

Benefits

  • Classic mogra (jasmine) fragrance that feels traditional and comforting
  • Temple-like ambience in seconds in your pooja room, living room or satsang space
  • Soft and calming, not a loud synthetic party spray
  • No ash, no smoke: spray, let it settle and start your pooja
  • Fragrance is subjective; intensity varies by person and room size

Ideal for

  • Daily Krishna Ji Pooja and aarti
  • Janmashtami: Krishna Janam, jhula pooja and midnight aarti
  • Bhajan and kirtan evenings, Hare Krishna kirtan and satsang
  • Bhagavad Gita path or reading
  • Radhashtami, Sharad Purnima, Kartik month and other Vaishnav festivals
  • Japa or meditation corners, kids' pooja time, family gatherings and Gita Jayanti
  • Homes and flats where burning a lot of incense is not practical
  • Gifting for Janmashtami visits, Griha Pravesh, new mandir setups and elders

How to use

  • Lightly dust or arrange your Krishna altar.
  • Hold the bottle upright and spray 3–6 times into the air around the room.
  • Lightly spray towards curtains and corners, and near (but not on) the mandir, from a safe distance.
  • Wait a minute for the fragrance to spread, then light the diya and begin your pooja.

Care and safety

  • Do not spray directly on idols or photos of deities, on diyas, candles, agarbatti or open flames, or on prasad and food.
  • Avoid spraying on skin, eyes or face.
  • Test on a small hidden area before using on delicate fabrics or surfaces.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat.
