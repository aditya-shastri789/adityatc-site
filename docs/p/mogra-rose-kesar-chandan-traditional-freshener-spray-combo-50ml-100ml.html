Siddhak Freshener Spray Combo brings four classic traditional fragrances together: Pooja Freshener, Mogra, Rose and Kesar Chandan. Whether you want the sweet floral lift of mogra, the fresh elegance of rose petals or the warm richness of kesar-chandan, there is a scent for every mood and moment.

Each spray releases a fine mist that quickly refreshes your surroundings at home, in the office or wherever you want your space to feel clean and welcoming.

Fragrances in the combo

  • Pooja Freshener: a fresh, all-purpose fragrance for the pooja room
  • Mogra (jasmine): sweet, floral, uplifting
  • Rose: fresh, soft floral with a lightly sweet finish
  • Kesar Chandan: warm saffron notes with smooth, creamy sandalwood

Benefits

  • Four traditional fragrances for different moods and spaces
  • Instant freshness: a quick spray helps reduce the perception of everyday odours
  • Fine mist spreads evenly for a smooth, balanced fragrance (not harsh)
  • Easy intensity control: spray lightly for a subtle scent or more for larger spaces

Where to use

  • Living rooms, bedrooms and entryways
  • Bathrooms and guest rooms
  • Offices, reception areas, salons and shops
  • Cars (spray into the air, not directly on surfaces)
  • Closets and small spaces

How to use

  • Shake well before use.
  • Hold upright and spray 2–4 times into the air.
  • Adjust the number of sprays to room size and the strength you want.
  • Spray away from the face and let the mist settle naturally.

Safety and care

  • For external use only. Do not ingest.
  • Avoid contact with eyes; rinse with water if contact occurs.
  • Keep away from heat, sparks and open flames.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

Pack sizes

  • 4 sprays × 50 ml (travel-friendly)
  • 4 sprays × 100 ml (home use)
