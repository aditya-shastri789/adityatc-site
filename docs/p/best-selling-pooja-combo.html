What's inside

  • Haldi Kumkum – 100 g: for tilak, aarti, prasad rituals and auspicious ceremonies

  • Nada Laccha – 2 packs: for tying the kalash, sealing pooja items and ritual arrangements
  • Janeu – 40 packs: for puja, havan, sankalp and religious ceremonies

  • Gugal Powder – 100 g: for dhoop / dhuni and ritual fragrance
  • Pitambari – 100 g: for cleaning brass and copper pooja utensils
