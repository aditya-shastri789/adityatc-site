Siddhak dhoop sticks for pooja combo brings together four naturally made dhoop sticks, 50 of each: Panchgavya, Guggul, Rose and Chandan (sandalwood). It is made for devotees who want a pure, devotional atmosphere at home for daily pooja, Gita reading, mantra chanting, meditation or yoga.

In Sanatan Dharma, lighting dhoop is a sacred offering: it is the "Dhupa" step of the Shodashopachara (16-step) pooja, and the rising smoke is seen as prayers reaching the divine. Devotees believe pure dhoop cleanses the atmosphere and supports sadhana, bhakti and meditation.

Benefits

  • Natural base materials (herbs, woods, resins, plant powders): no charcoal, no harmful chemicals, no synthetic binders
  • Softer, more natural, temple-like fragrances, suitable around elders and children and for people sensitive to harsh incense
  • A different fragrance for each deity or mood, for example sandalwood for Vishnu / Krishna and guggul for Shiva
  • Keeps your daily pooja fresh rather than repetitive
  • Many people feel calmer and more focused during prayer and meditation (traditional perspective)

Ideal for

  • Morning and evening aarti in the home mandir
  • Japa of "Om Namah Shivaya", "Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya" or the Gayatri Mantra
  • Reading the Gita, Ramayana, Bhagavatam or Upanishads; meditation and pranayama; satsang and kirtan
  • Diwali, Dhanteras, Lakshmi Pooja, Navratri, Durga Pooja, Maha Shivratri, Janmashtami, Ram Navami and Ganesh Chaturthi
  • Satyanarayan Katha, Griha Pooja, Griha Pravesh, office or shop opening
  • Gifting to spiritual family members, newly married couples and friends, at housewarmings and festivals

How to use

  • Clean the pooja area and arrange deity photos or murtis, diya, flowers and water.
  • Choose a fragrance for the deity, mood or occasion.
  • Hold the stick at the bare end and light the coated end with a matchstick or diya.
  • Let it burn for a few seconds until the tip glows, then gently blow out the flame.
  • Wave the dhoop in a slow circle before the deity, chanting "Om Shanti Shanti Shantihi", "Om Namo Narayanaya" or "Om Gan Ganapataye Namah".
  • Place it in a proper stand or fire-safe base where falling ash lands safely, then sit for aarti, japa or meditation.

Safety and storage

  • Always place burning dhoop in a stable, non-flammable holder.
  • Keep away from curtains, paper, furniture and flammable decor.
  • Do not leave dhoop unattended, especially around children or pets.
  • Use in a well-ventilated area, particularly in small rooms.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture and sunlight, and reseal the pack.
