Siddhak organic dhoop masala (100 g) is a loose dhoop powder for those who value purity and tradition. Burning natural dhoop is a traditional way to clear heaviness from a room and fill it with a calm, pleasant fragrance, whether at home, in the office, a studio or a spiritual space.

It is made from natural ingredients such as aromatic woods, resins, herbs, flowers and plant powders, with no charcoal, synthetic fillers, artificial fragrance or toxic chemicals. It burns with a softer, more natural aroma, which suits people who find stick incense too harsh.

Benefits

  • Traditionally used to dispel negativity and create a light, happy atmosphere
  • Supports peace and focus during meditation, mantra chanting, yoga, reading or deep work
  • Completes daily aarti, havan or spiritual practice with a natural fragrance
  • Chemical-free and gentle on the senses
  • Economical: 100 g gives many sessions, and you control how much to use each time (a pinch or a spoonful)
  • Effects are based on traditional belief; individual experience may vary

Ideal for

  • Daily pooja and aarti, morning and evening
  • Meditation and yoga
  • Clearing the air after stressful days
  • New beginnings: a new home, new job or new project
  • Before or after guests and gatherings
  • Homes, offices, yoga studios, healing spaces and personal altars

How to use

  • With a charcoal tablet: light the tablet in a heat-safe metal or clay burner until it glows, then sprinkle a small pinch of dhoop masala on top. Add more in small amounts as needed.
  • On a traditional dhoop burner (dhoop dani): add a thin layer of ash or sand, place a burning ember safely inside, and add a little dhoop masala so it slowly smoulders.
  • In an electric dhoop / incense diffuser (if compatible): add a small amount to the tray and switch on as per the device instructions.
  • Simple ritual: tidy the room, open a window for a moment, light the dhoop, set an intention ("May this space be filled with peace, love, light and positive energy"), let the fragrance reach every corner and sit quietly for a few moments.

Safety and storage

  • Always burn in a heat-resistant holder or burner, in a well-ventilated area.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains and flammable items, and never leave burning dhoop unattended.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture and sunlight, and reseal the pack after each use.
