Siddhak Pure Organic Manure for plants is a nutrient-rich blend that improves soil fertility and supports healthier, stronger plant growth, for home gardeners and farmers alike.

Unlike chemical fertilisers that give quick results but harm soil over time, organic manure works with nature, improving soil structure, moisture retention and microbial activity. It is slowly decomposed by natural processes to lock in nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium along with essential micronutrients.

Benefits

  • 100% organic and chemical-free: safe for all plants, soil types and the environment
  • Improves soil structure: better aeration and water-holding capacity
  • Boosts microbial activity that supports growth
  • Slow, steady nutrient release with no risk of burning plants
  • Odour-controlled and professionally cured for indoor and outdoor use
  • Helps plants develop stronger roots and greener leaves, for better yield
  • An eco-friendly choice for those moving to organic cultivation

Where to use

  • Vegetable gardens, kitchen gardens and herbs
  • Fruit trees and flowering plants
  • Lawns and landscaping
  • Potted indoor and outdoor plants
  • Organic farming fields and nursery plantations

How to use

  • Mix the manure into the topsoil, 1–2 inches deep.
  • Use during soil preparation, at planting, or as a regular nutrient booster.
  • Apply once every 20–30 days for best results.

Pack sizes

  • 10 kg bag: home gardens, terrace gardens, pots, indoor plants, kitchen gardens and small landscaping
  • 50 kg bag: farm fields, nurseries, commercial growers, large landscaping and bulk needs
