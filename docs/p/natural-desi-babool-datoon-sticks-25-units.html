Siddhak Desi Babool datoon (datun) sticks are a simple, time-tested way to care for your teeth and gums, just like our elders did. Before plastic toothbrushes and flavoured toothpastes, Indian homes used Babool (Acacia) twigs to keep the mouth clean and fresh.

Babool has traditionally been valued in Ayurveda for oral hygiene. Each stick is 100% natural Babool wood: no foaming agents, no artificial colours, no synthetic flavours.

Benefits

  • Natural oral care: the traditional way to gently clean teeth and support gum hygiene
  • Chewing the twig releases natural juices that help freshen breath and reduce food deposits
  • Eco-friendly and plastic-free: no plastic handle or nylon bristles; completely biodegradable
  • Chemical-free routine: no SLS, parabens, artificial sweeteners or synthetic fragrance
  • Minimalist: one stick is enough for a full brushing session
  • Cost-effective: 25 sticks give many days of use at a low cost per use
  • Fits a natural, satvik, Ayurveda-inspired lifestyle

Ideal for

  • People who prefer natural and Ayurveda-inspired products
  • Anyone cutting down on plastic, and eco-conscious families teaching kids sustainable living
  • Yogis, meditators and spiritual seekers
  • Bathroom, travel bag, office, farmhouse, village home or ashram
  • Religious fasts, rituals and natural-only days

How to use

  • Take one stick and gently chew one end until it forms soft, brush-like fibres.
  • Clean your teeth in small circular motions and gently massage your gums.
  • Spit out any residue and rinse with clean water.
  • Cut off the used end and keep the rest for later, or discard the stick after one full use.
  • Best used daily in the morning. You can use it alongside your regular brush.
  • New to datoon? Start a few times a week to get used to the taste and feel.

Storage

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture, and use a fresh stick regularly for hygiene.
