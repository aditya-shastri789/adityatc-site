Siddhak lotus dhoop sticks bring the gentle energy of the lotus into your home temple. Maa Lakshmi is shown seated on a fully bloomed lotus, holding lotus flowers and surrounded by lotus blooms, and in Hindu belief the lotus (Padma) stands for prosperity, purity of heart and spiritual upliftment. Lighting lotus dhoop is a symbolic offering of respect and gratitude to the Goddess.

Benefits

  • Made from natural base materials with a lotus-inspired aroma
  • No harsh chemicals, synthetic binders or overpowering artificial perfume
  • Gentle, floral, calming fragrance: not too sharp, not too smoky
  • Helps your pooja room feel like a small temple and supports focus during japa and prayer
  • Lotus and Lakshmi are associated with prosperity, peace and abundance in Hindu culture (traditional belief; experiences differ)

Ideal for

  • Daily Lakshmi, Lakshmi–Narayan or Lakshmi–Ganesh pooja
  • Diwali and Dhanteras
  • Friday (Shukravar) vrat and pooja
  • Kojagiri / Sharad Purnima Lakshmi Pooja and Varalakshmi Vratam
  • New business openings, shop and office pooja, cash counters and financial beginnings
  • Griha Pravesh and vastu shanti
  • Evening aarti with "Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah", Lakshmi Ashtottara Shatanamavali or Sri Suktam

How to use

  • Clean the mandir and place Maa Lakshmi's murti or photo (often with Lord Ganesha and/or Lord Vishnu), with diya, flowers, kumkum, haldi, rice, sweets and water.
  • Hold one stick at the wooden / bare end and light the other tip with a matchstick or diya flame.
  • Let it burn for a few seconds, then gently blow out the flame so it smoulders.
  • Wave the dhoop in slow circles before the Goddess, then place it in a dhoop stand or fire-safe holder.
  • Continue with Lakshmi aarti, mantra chanting or Sri Suktam.

Safety and storage

  • Always place a burning dhoop stick in a stable, non-flammable holder on a heat-resistant surface.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains, paper and other flammable items.
  • Use in a well-ventilated area, especially in small rooms.
  • Never leave burning dhoop unattended.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight, and keep the pack closed to preserve the fragrance.
