Siddhak 100% natural firewood ash for garden and farm use is sourced from pure, untreated firewood. Gardeners and farmers use it for its mineral content and its soil pH-balancing and natural pest-repelling properties, whether for vegetables, flowers, indoor plants or fruit trees.

Firewood ash has been used in traditional and modern farming for decades. It contains potassium (K), which supports plant strength, drought tolerance and flowering, along with calcium, magnesium and micronutrients that help maintain soil fertility and strong root development. Our ash is finely powdered so it mixes easily into soil.

Benefits

  • 100% natural and chemical-free: from pure firewood with no additives
  • Rich in potassium and minerals: supports strong roots, better flowering and healthier growth
  • Improves soil structure: aids aeration, reduces acidity and supports microbial activity
  • Natural pest repellent: helps keep slugs, ants and harmful insects away
  • For all plants: vegetables, fruit trees, flowering plants, indoor pots, terrace gardens and farms
  • Eco-friendly and sustainable, finely powdered for easy mixing with soil or compost

How to use

  • Pots and indoor plants: mix 1–2 tablespoons into the topsoil once every 2–3 weeks.
  • Garden beds: spread a thin layer and mix lightly into the soil before watering.
  • Composting: add a small amount to enrich compost with minerals.
  • Fruit trees and vegetables: apply around the base to support flowering and yield.
  • Avoid excessive use, as too much ash can make soil alkaline.

Pack sizes

  • 500 g: for small home gardens, balcony plants and regular care
  • 1000 g (1 kg): for large gardens, farms or heavy soil improvement
