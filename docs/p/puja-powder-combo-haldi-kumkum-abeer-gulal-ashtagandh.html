Siddhak Puja Powder Combo brings the four powders every pooja thali needs, in one pack:

  • Ujjain Special Haldi Kumkum, 100 g
  • Kesar Ashtagandh (Chandan tilak powder), 100 g
  • Abeer, 25 g
  • Gulal, 25 g

In Sanatan Dharma, applying kumkum and ashtagandh tilak and offering abeer and gulal are part of daily pooja and of festivals like Navratri, Dussehra, Karwa Chauth and Diwali Lakshmi pooja. Ashtagandh is offered to Bhagwan Vishnu and used as a tilak; haldi kumkum is offered to Mata and worn by married women.

Benefits

  • Everything for tilak and the pooja thali in one order
  • Packed in sealed jars, easy to store and use
  • Traditional formulation from Ujjain
  • A good gift for festivals

How to use: take a pinch with the ring finger for tilak, or offer to the deity during pooja. Keep the jars closed and dry.
