Siddhak Natural Wellness Kit Combo is a set of three everyday essentials for gentle daily care: skin care, oral care and personal grooming in one value pack. A good start for a chemical-free self-care routine, and an easy wellness gift.

What's inside

  • Natural earbuds – pack of 80: soft cotton tips on firm, durable sticks; individually packed; for gentle outer-ear cleaning, makeup fixes, nail polish touch-ups and first-aid
  • Pure Multani Mitti (Fuller's Earth): free from harmful chemicals and artificial fragrance; cleanses pores and removes excess oil; helps skin look clearer and brighter and reduces the look of tan; for face pack, body pack or hair mask; especially suited to oily and combination skin
  • Nano bristle toothbrush: ultra-soft, densely packed nano bristles that clean between teeth and along the gum line; gentle on sensitive teeth and gums; helps remove plaque without harsh scrubbing; for kids, adults and the elderly

Benefits

  • 3-in-1 daily care: no need to buy separate products
  • Natural and gentle on skin, teeth and gums
  • Compact and useful for all ages: a gift, travel kit or starter self-care pack

How to use (Multani Mitti face pack)

  • Mix Multani Mitti with rose water or plain water to a smooth paste.
  • Apply on a clean face, avoiding eyes and lips, let it dry, then rinse with lukewarm water.
