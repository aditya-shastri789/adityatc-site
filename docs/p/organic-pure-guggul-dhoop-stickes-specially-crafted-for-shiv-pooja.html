Siddhak pure guggal dhoop sticks fill your pooja room with the ancient fragrance of guggal (guggul), a resin burned for centuries in Shiv temples, Jyotirlingas, ashrams and havan kunds. They are made for devotees who want to perform Shiv Pooja in a traditional, sattvik way.

From Rudrabhishek on Mondays to Maha Shivratri, Shravan month and Pradosh Vrat, guggal dhoop is closely tied to the worship of Mahadev. As per Hindu belief, guggal smoke purifies the surroundings and removes negative vibrations, and many purohits and sadhus prefer it for Shiv Pooja.

Benefits

  • Cow dung (gobar) base with natural guggal resin: no charcoal, no harmful chemicals, no synthetic fragrance
  • Deep, slightly sweet, resinous temple aroma that recalls havan and yajna
  • Supports a focused mind while chanting "Om Namah Shivaya", the Mahamrityunjaya Mantra or Rudram Chamakam
  • Suits home mandirs, homes with elders and children, and people sensitive to harsh, chemical incense
  • Many devotees feel a greater sense of peace and a more temple-like aura at home (a matter of faith)

Ideal for

  • Daily morning and evening Shiv aarti and Shivling pooja at home
  • Mondays (Somvar) and Rudrabhishek with milk, bilva leaves, honey, curd, ghee, sugar and water
  • Shravan / Sawan month, Maha Shivratri jagran and Pradosh Vrat
  • Meditation, pranayama, Mahamrityunjaya japa and reading the Shiva Purana or Rudram

How to use

  • Clean the altar and arrange the Shivling or Mahadev's photo, water, panchamrit, belpatra, flowers and diya.
  • Hold the stick at the wooden / bare end and light the resin-coated tip with a matchstick or diya.
  • Let it burn for a few seconds until the tip glows, then gently blow out the flame so it smoulders.
  • Wave the dhoop in gentle clockwise circles before the Shivling while chanting "Om Namah Shivaya" or "Tryambakam Yajamahe...".
  • Place it in a proper dhoop stand on a heat-resistant surface, then continue with abhishek, belpatra, flowers and aarti.

Safety and storage

  • Always place a burning dhoop stick in a stable, non-flammable holder on a heat-resistant surface.
  • Keep away from children, pets, curtains, paper and other flammable items.
  • Use in a well-ventilated area, especially in small rooms.
  • Never leave burning dhoop unattended.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight, and keep the pack closed to preserve the fragrance.
